# Species Audit: Dragonborn

## Summary

No `race`-type Item exists for Dragonborn (`raceItemExists: false`), matching the cross-species finding that all 9 core PHB 2024 species failed to get a race item while 7 non-core species succeeded. All 13 trait items have byte-for-byte empty `effects`/`activities`/`advancement` — the raw placeholder shape `buildRaceTraitItemData` initializes (`scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:182-209`), consistent with an import that predates the 2026-08-25 detector commits. But Dragonborn is a worse case than most other species even on a fresh re-import against current HEAD: the marquee **Breath Weapon** trait would come out actively wrong, not just incomplete (correct DC formula, but a misattributed *uses* formula, a lost damage die, and a lost cone-or-line choice), and **Draconic Ancestry** — the trait every other Dragonborn mechanic depends on — has no representation whatsoever under current dnd5e advancement types, so Damage Resistance's and Breath Weapon's damage types can never be resolved automatically no matter how good the prose parser gets. The 13 traits are also not 13 distinct real traits: three names are duplicated (**Breath Weapon**, **Damage Resistance**, **Draconic Ancestry**), and a fourth pair differs only in name (**Ability Score Increase** / **Ability Score Increases**) — in every case one copy is unmistakably 2014-legacy phrasing and the other is 2024 phrasing, both present simultaneously because `usableRacialTraits` (`scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:165-169`) filters only on `hideInSheet`, not on ruleset. A re-import today would grant a character both rulesets' versions of four separate mechanics at once.

## Traits

### Darkvision
- **Prose:** "You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet."
- **Expected (current code):** Matches cleanly. `scanSegmentText`'s darkvision regex (`scripts/scraper.mjs:752-758`) matches "Darkvision with a range of 60 feet" via its `with...a range of` connector branch, producing `effectHints.push({ key: "system.attributes.senses.darkvision", mode: 4 (upgrade), value: "60" })`. `guessQueueEntries` (`scripts/effects-builder.mjs:899-913`) turns this into one transfer:true ActiveEffect.
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty — consistent with staleness, not a detector gap.
- **Gap / verdict:** No gap. A fresh reimport on current HEAD would correctly attach a Darkvision-60 effect.

### Breath Weapon (duplicated — 2024 and 2014 phrasings both present)
- **Prose (2024, item `3fbkvjZAJdO5dD5d`):** "When you take the Attack action..., you can replace one of your attacks with an exhalation... in either a 15-foot Cone or a 30-foot Line... Each creature in that area must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 plus your Constitution modifier and Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, a creature takes 1d10 damage of the type determined by your Draconic Ancestry trait... increases by 1d10 at levels 5/11/17... a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, ... regain all expended uses ... Long Rest."
- **Prose (2014, item `dbVmCjezzB8bzJOm`):** "You can use your action to exhale destructive energy... each creature ... must make a saving throw, the type of which is determined by your draconic ancestry. The DC ... equals 8 + your Constitution modifier + your proficiency bonus. A creature takes 2d6 damage on a failed save... increases to 3d6/4d6/5d6 at 6th/11th/16th level... you can't use it again until you complete a short or long rest."
- **Expected (current code) — this is the richest case in the whole species, verified by directly executing `scanSegmentText` and `buildActivityData` against both texts:**
  - **2024 text** produces `savingThrow: { ability: "dex", dcFormula: "8 + @abilities.con.mod + @prof", onSaveHalf: true }` — `parseDcFormula` (`scripts/scraper.mjs:276-302`, issue 016) gets this exactly right. `parseActivation` (`scripts/scraper.mjs:322-337`) reads the leading "When you take the Attack action..." clause and returns `type: "special", condition: "When you take the Attack action on your turn"` — a defensible fit given dnd5e has no built-in "replaces one of your attacks" activation type. `parseRangeAndTarget` (`scripts/scraper.mjs:352-371`) finds `target.template = { type: "cone", size: "15", units: "ft" }` — **but the regex (`scripts/scraper.mjs:358`) is a single non-global `.match()`, so it only ever captures the *first* area phrase found; the "30-foot Line" alternative is silently discarded.** A generated activity would only ever apply a cone template, never letting the player pick Line.
  - **The 1d10 base damage is never captured at all.** `diceRe` (`scripts/scraper.mjs:489-490`) caps its trailing "context" capture group at 25 characters (`[A-Za-z][A-Za-z ]{0,25}?`) before requiring a stopping punctuation/"and"/"or". The actual text after "1d10" is "damage of the type determined by your Draconic Ancestry trait." — 60+ characters to the next period — so the regex never finds a valid stopping point and the whole dice mention is skipped. Confirmed by direct execution: `diceHints` comes back `[]`. The resulting Save activity's `damage.parts` is an empty array — a Breath Weapon activity with a save, a DC, and a cone template, but **zero damage**.
  - **`usesFormula` is wrong, not just missing.** `scanSegmentText`'s uses-formula detector (`scripts/scraper.mjs:439-441`) matches the *first* `"<Ability> modifier"` phrase anywhere in the whole segment — here that's "Constitution modifier" from the DC clause — and unconditionally treats it as the uses-pool formula. There is no detector at all for "a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus" as a *uses count* (only as a DC term, via `parseDcFormula`, or a *damage* multiplier, via the `profRe` dice-modifier scanner). Verified by direct execution: `usesFormula` comes back `"@abilities.con.mod"`, so the generated activity's `uses.max` would be Constitution modifier — completely wrong; the real rule is "= your Proficiency Bonus." `recoveryPeriod` does come out correctly as `"lr"` (Long Rest).
  - Net simulated result (built with `buildActivityData("save", ...)` directly against the current code): a Save-type activity with `save.ability: ["dex"]`, `save.dc: { calculation: "formula", formula: "8 + @abilities.con.mod + @prof" }` (correct), `damage.onSave: "half"` (correct), `damage.parts: []` (wrong — empty), `uses.max: "@abilities.con.mod"` (wrong — should be `@prof`), `uses.recovery: [{period: "lr", ...}]` (correct), `target.template: {type:"cone", size:"15"}` (partially correct — Line choice lost), `activation: {type:"special", condition:"When you take the Attack action on your turn"}` (a reasonable but imperfect fit).
  - **2014 text is worse: no save is detected at all.** `scanSegmentText`'s saving-throw regex (`scripts/scraper.mjs:460`, `/\b(Strength|Dexterity|...)\s+saving throw\b/i`) requires the ability name to sit directly before the literal words "saving throw." The 2014 phrasing defers the ability ("must make a saving throw, the type of which is determined by your draconic ancestry") — no named ability is ever adjacent to "saving throw," so `savingThrow` comes back `null`. `guessQueueEntries` (`scripts/effects-builder.mjs:853-869`) falls into its `else` branch (plain damage, no save) whenever `savingThrow` is null, so the 2d6 die (which *is* captured correctly this time — the trailing context "damage on a failed save" is short enough to match) gets built as a bare **Damage** activity with no saving throw and no half-on-success at all — i.e., current code would generate an activity that just deals 2d6 damage unconditionally, which is a materially wrong representation of a save-based ability. It also mis-parses recovery: the text says "short or long rest," but `srIdx`/`lrIdx` (`scripts/scraper.mjs:432-436`) only look for the literal contiguous phrases `"short rest"` and `"long rest"` — since the text reads "short **or long** rest" (no contiguous "short rest" substring), only the "long rest" branch matches, silently collapsing a short-or-long recovery into Long-Rest-only.
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty for both copies, consistent with staleness.
- **Gap / verdict:** Even fully re-imported on current HEAD, **neither** Breath Weapon copy would come out functionally correct. This is new territory beyond issues 016/017: (1) `diceRe`'s 25-char context cap is too short whenever the sentence defers the damage type to "as determined by X" phrasing — very likely to recur on any per-choice damage-type trait, not just Dragonborn; (2) the uses-formula detector has no support for "a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus," and worse, it false-positives on an unrelated ability-modifier mention from the DC clause with no proximity/relevance check; (3) `parseRangeAndTarget`'s area regex has no support for an "either X or Y" shape choice; (4) the saving-throw detector requires the ability name to be literally adjacent to "saving throw," which fails on any phrasing (like the 2014 PHB's own) that names the ability indirectly; (5) the rest-recovery detector can't parse "short or long rest" as a combined phrase. None of these are covered by issues 001-019.

### Damage Resistance (duplicated — near-identical 2014/2024 phrasing)
- **Prose:** "You have resistance to the damage type associated with your draconic ancestry." / "You have Resistance to the damage type determined by your Draconic Ancestry trait." (both copies, functionally identical)
- **Expected (current code):** No effect is produced, correctly. `traitRe` (`scripts/scraper.mjs:582-590`) technically pattern-matches "resistance to the damage" (with "the" as the captured filler word before the literal "damage"), but the captured type ("the") is checked against `DAMAGE_TYPE_WORDS` (`scripts/scraper.mjs:28-31`) and rejected since "the" isn't a real damage type — verified directly. So no `system.traits.dr.value` change is ever queued.
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty.
- **Gap / verdict:** Correctly produces nothing — but not because a phrasing fix would help. This trait's damage type is inherently a **runtime value chosen elsewhere** (Draconic Ancestry), which the flat `system.traits.dr.value` effect key can't represent as a formula/reference — see Species-level notes. Not a prose-parsing gap; a structural one.

### Speed
- **Prose:** "Your speed is 30 ft."
- **Expected (current code):** No effect is produced. All three of `scanSegmentText`'s speed regexes (`scripts/scraper.mjs:712,724,736`) require either a delta ("speed increases by/to N feet") or an explicit "you have a `<type>` speed of N feet" phrasing — verified directly that "Your speed is 30 ft." matches none of them (no "increases," no "you have a"). This trait's number is meant to come from `raceDef.weightSpeeds?.normal` on the race item (`assembleRaceItem`, `scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:326`) and/or `mergeTraitDerivedMovement` (`scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:288-315`), neither of which is trait-prose-driven for the flat "current speed" case, and neither of which ever runs for Dragonborn since the race item is never created.
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty.
- **Gap / verdict:** No gap on the trait item — this is redundant flavor text once a race item exists. It's only "missing" here because of finding #1 (no race item), same root cause as every other core-species Speed trait.

### Draconic Ancestry (duplicated — 2014 and 2024 phrasings, different table shapes)
- **Prose (2024):** "Choose the kind of dragon from the Draconic Ancestors table. Your choice affects your Breath Weapon and Damage Resistance traits..." + a Dragon→Damage Type table.
- **Prose (2014):** Same framing + a Dragon→Damage Type→Breath Weapon shape/save table (richer — also encodes which ancestries use a Dex vs. Con save and cone vs. line, information the 2024 write-up moved elsewhere).
- **Expected (current code):** Nothing. Verified by direct execution: no dice, no saving throw, no proficiency/training/language clause, no ASI phrasing — every detector in `scanSegmentText`/`guessProficiencyAdvancement`/`guessAbilityScoreImprovement` returns empty/null against both versions of this text.
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty — matches expectation exactly (not a staleness artifact; this would still be empty today).
- **Gap / verdict:** This is the single most consequential gap in the whole species — see Species-level notes below. It isn't a missing regex; there is no dnd5e advancement type this choice can be mapped onto at all under the current pipeline.

### Size
- **Prose:** "Your size is Medium (about 5–7 feet tall)."
- **Expected (current code):** No detector in `scanSegmentText` matches "Your size is..." phrasing at all — there is no size detector anywhere in the file. The real Size mechanic is meant to live on the race item as a `Size`-type advancement entry built by `buildSizeAdvancement` (`scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:248-265`) from `raceDef.sizeId` (structured DDB data, not this trait's prose), assembled into `assembleRaceItem` (`scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:324-362`).
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty.
- **Gap / verdict:** No gap on the trait item. Another casualty of finding #1 — `buildSizeAdvancement` never runs for Dragonborn because `importDdbRace` never reaches race-item assembly.

### Creature Type
- **Prose:** "You are a Humanoid."
- **Expected (current code):** No detector produces anything from this trait's own text — there is no creature-type detector anywhere in `scraper.mjs`. `assembleRaceItem` unconditionally hardcodes `system.type.value: "humanoid"` on the race item (`scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:346`) regardless of any trait's prose, per the code's own comment that playable species are Humanoid by default under both rulesets.
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty.
- **Gap / verdict:** No gap — this is inherently non-mechanical flavor text for a `feat`-type item; the actual mechanic lives on the (currently nonexistent) race item, which would in fact get `humanoid` correctly if it existed.

### Ability Score Increase / Ability Score Increases (duplicated — 2014 flat and 2024 floating phrasings)
- **Prose (2014, "Ability Score Increase"):** "Your Strength score increases by 2, and your Charisma score increases by 1."
- **Prose (2024, "Ability Score Increases"):** "When determining your character's ability scores, increase one score by 2 and a different one by 1, or increase three scores by 1. None of these increases can raise a score above 20."
- **Expected (current code):** **Neither produces an advancement entry.** `guessAbilityScoreImprovement` (`scripts/scraper.mjs:881-910`) anchors its extraction regex (line 884) on `Increase\s+(?:your|one ability score of your choice|two ability scores of your choice)` — a phrasing family drawn from feats/Epic Boons ("Increase your Constitution score...", "Increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or increase two ability scores of your choice by 1..."). Verified directly: the 2014 text never contains the literal word "Increase" at the start of the relevant sentence (it says "Your Strength score **increases**..."), and the 2024 species-level text says "increase **one score** by 2..." — never "one **ability score of your choice**" — so neither matches, and `sentence` is `undefined` in both cases, returning `null`. `guessProficiencyAdvancement` doesn't apply either (no proficiency/training/language language present).
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty for both copies.
- **Gap / verdict:** Genuine detector gap, not just staleness — even on current HEAD, Dragonborn (and by the same logic every other 2024 species, since this is the standard 2024 species-ASI boilerplate, not Dragonborn-specific wording) would still get **zero** ASI advancement from either trait copy. Issue 009 only wired the *existing* detector into `buildAutoMechanics`; it never extended its phrasing coverage to the species-level sentence shape. Not covered by any of issues 001-019 — worth its own issue. Separately, having both a flat 2014 (+2 Str/+1 Cha) and a floating 2024 (2024 ASI-choice) trait simultaneously present is itself a data problem (see Species-level notes) — if the phrasing gap above were ever fixed without also fixing trait de-duplication, a re-imported Dragonborn would receive **both** ASI grants at once.

### Draconic Flight
- **Prose:** "When you reach character level 5, ... As a Bonus Action, you sprout spectral wings ... that last for 10 minutes ... During that time, you have a Fly Speed equal to your Speed. ... Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest."
- **Expected (current code):** Nothing is queued, verified by direct execution. `parseActivation` correctly identifies `type: "bonus"` (matches "As a Bonus Action," `BONUS_ACTION_PHRASE_RE`, `scripts/scraper.mjs:312`) and captures a `condition` — but that condition ends up being the *leading* clause "When you reach character level 5" (`TRIGGER_CLAUSE_RE`, `scripts/scraper.mjs:320`, anchored to the start of the segment), which is a level-gate/prerequisite, not a genuine per-use trigger; it would show on the built activity as if reaching level 5 were the activation trigger every time. `usesFormula`/`recoveryPeriod` correctly come out as `"1"` / `"lr"` (from "Once you use this trait... Long Rest"). But none of this is ever attached to anything: `guessQueueEntries` (`scripts/effects-builder.mjs:831-966`) only builds an activity/effect from a segment that has a saving throw, dice hint, spell reference, or an ActiveEffect-shaped keyword match (AC/HP/resistance/speed/darkvision/condition) — Draconic Flight has none of those. "You have a Fly Speed equal to your Speed" is a **relative/derived** grant (fly speed copies whatever the character's current speed is), a fundamentally different shape from every speed regex in `scanSegmentText` (`scripts/scraper.mjs:712-740`), all of which only recognize a literal "N feet" absolute or delta value. So the activation/uses/recovery data that *was* correctly parsed is simply discarded — nothing is ever built.
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty — matches expectation for the "no fly-speed grant" half, though not for the reason a naive read would suggest (it's not "no detector even tried," it's "the detector's own findings had nowhere to go").
- **Gap / verdict:** New gap, not covered by issues 001-019: no detector represents "grant a movement type equal to your existing Speed" — a relative-value speed grant. This is arguably the second-most mechanically interesting trait in the species (after Breath Weapon) and it currently produces nothing at all, despite most of its underlying facts (bonus action, 1 use, Long Rest recovery) already being correctly extracted and then thrown away for lack of a mechanic to attach them to.

## Species-level notes

**Race item absence (finding #1).** Confirmed for Dragonborn: `raceItemExists: false`. `importDdbRace` (`scripts/importer.mjs:929-963`) wraps the entire trait-creation loop *and* the subsequent `buildSizeAdvancement`/`mergeTraitDerivedMovement`/`assembleRaceItem` call in one `try`/`catch`, but `createItemFromData` (`scripts/importer.mjs:371-403`) — which is what actually calls `Item.create` for the race item itself — has its **own** internal `try`/`catch` that reports failures via `report.failed` rather than re-throwing. That means a race-item creation failure would *not* normally abort anything upstream, and doesn't explain why zero race items exist for exactly the 9 core species while all their trait items persisted successfully. The more likely explanation, visible directly in this species' own duplicated trait data: for these 9 core PHB species, D&D Beyond's API appears to return **both a legacy (2014) and a current (2024) race entry** under the same species name, and both get processed as if they were one species (see below) — sharing this audit's evidence but not something this read-only pass can fully confirm without instrumenting a live import and inspecting `report.failed`.

**Duplicate traits are a real legacy/2024 double-import, not an accidental re-run.** Four trait names appear twice (three identical-name pairs plus the Increase/Increases pair), and in every single case the two copies have **materially different mechanical content**, not identical text: Breath Weapon's 2024 copy uses 1d10 dice scaling at 5/11/17 with uses = Proficiency Bonus recharging on a Long Rest, while its 2014 copy uses 2d6 scaling at 6/11/16 with a flat single use recharging on a short **or** long rest; Draconic Ancestry's 2024 table has two columns (Dragon, Damage Type) while its 2014 table has three (adding Breath Weapon shape/save); the ASI pair is the classic 2014 flat "+2 Str/+1 Cha" vs. the 2024 floating "2-and-1-or-three-1s" boilerplate. This rules out "same import ran twice" (which would produce byte-identical duplicates) and confirms `usableRacialTraits` (`scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:165-169`) is receiving racial-trait data spanning **both rulesets** for Dragonborn and passing all of it through with no `isLegacy`/ruleset filter — every other function downstream (`buildRaceTraitItemData`'s own `isLegacy` parameter, `sourceField`) is clearly built to distinguish the two, but nothing upstream decides which one the import should actually keep. Left unfixed, a working re-import (even with every detector gap above fixed) would grant a Dragonborn character both a flat and a floating ASI, two independently-recharging Breath Weapon activities, and two redundant Damage Resistance effects simultaneously.

**Draconic Ancestry choice modeling — no current advancement type fits.** Draconic Ancestry is a "choose one of 10 named options, and that choice supplies a value two *other* traits both depend on" pattern. Checking dnd5e's advancement types actually present in this codebase's usage (`AbilityScoreImprovement`, `ItemGrant`, `Size`, `Trait` — see `scripts/scraper.mjs:917-985` and `scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:218-265`): `Trait` grants real proficiencies/languages/tools from a fixed vocabulary, not an arbitrary enum; `ItemGrant`/`ItemChoice` grant/choose whole embedded Items, not a scalar value another item's `system.traits.dr.value` or a damage-part's `type` field could read back out — dnd5e has no roll-data-style cross-item reference for "whichever damage type this other choice resolved to." The only mechanically-correct way to model this today would be to abandon the shared generic Breath Weapon/Damage Resistance items entirely and instead author **10 self-contained variant pairs** (e.g. "Breath Weapon (Black)" with acid damage and a line template, "Breath Weapon (Gold)" with fire and a cone, ...) and grant exactly one pair via an `ItemChoice` advancement keyed to the ancestry choice — a data-authoring change, not a parser fix. None of issues 001-019 addresses this, and it's out of scope for `scanSegmentText`-style prose detection no matter how it's extended.
