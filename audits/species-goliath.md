# Species Audit: Goliath

## Summary

No `race`-type item exists for Goliath in the compendium (`raceItemExists: false`) — only the 12 trait `feat` items exist, consistent with the pack-wide finding across all 9 core PHB 2024 species. Every trait's `effects`/`activities`/`advancement` are empty, and for most of that the 2026-08-24 import predates issues 011-015, so it's pure staleness. But Goliath has more real, still-open pipeline gaps than most species: **Ability Score Increases** hits the same cross-species ASI-boilerplate miss already found on Orc; **Giant Ancestry** (the richest choice-trait in the set) loses real mechanics to three separate, previously-undocumented bugs (a too-short regex context window, a segment-labeling/anchor interaction, and a shared-preamble-doesn't-propagate structural gap); **Large Form** would, if re-imported today, build one spurious *permanent* +10ft speed effect instead of anything resembling its actual bonus-action/temporary-size mechanic; and **both Powerful Build and Ability Score Increase appear twice**, each pair with genuinely different text — strong evidence that D&D Beyond's `racialTraits` payload for this species mixes 2014- and 2024-ruleset trait definitions with no per-trait filtering anywhere in this pipeline.

Note on repo state: `scripts/scraper.mjs`, `scripts/effects-builder.mjs`, and `scripts/importer.mjs` have uncommitted working-tree changes that already implement issues 016 (DC formula / uses-recovery wiring) and 017 (activation/range/target/toggle linking) — not yet committed (`git log` HEAD is `e160cc0`), but this is what's actually on disk and what a live import would run today. Issues 018 (Magic Initiate class narrowing) and 019 (always-prepared spell name detection) are spellcasting-specific and irrelevant to Goliath's traits. All findings below evaluate "current code" as this on-disk working-tree state, verified directly by calling `scanSegmentText`/`parseActivation`/`parseRangeAndTarget`/`guessProficiencyAdvancement`/`guessAbilityScoreAdvancement` from `scripts/scraper.mjs` against each trait's actual text (a small Node probe script, since `guessFeatureMechanics`'s DOMParser wrapper needs a browser) — not just manual regex reading.

## Traits

### Giant Ancestry
- **Prose:** "You are descended from Giants. Choose one of the following benefits... you can use the chosen benefit a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest," followed by six named options: **Cloud's Jaunt** (Bonus Action, teleport 30 ft), **Fire's Burn** (on a hit, +1d10 Fire damage), **Frost's Chill** (on a hit, +1d6 Cold damage and target's Speed −10 ft until your next turn), **Hill's Tumble** (on a hit vs. Large-or-smaller, inflict Prone), **Stone's Endurance** (Reaction, roll 1d12 + Constitution modifier, reduce damage taken by that much), **Storm's Thunder** (Reaction, deal 1d8 Thunder damage to an attacker within 60 ft).
- **Expected (current code):** This is Goliath's Draconic-Ancestry-equivalent, and it fares far worse under the current scanner than a simple flat trait. `guessFeatureMechanics` (scraper.mjs:833-865) splits the description at each `<p>`; each of the six options opens with `<strong>Name (Type).</strong>`, so `leadingBoldLabel` (scraper.mjs:193-206) correctly starts a new labeled segment per option — but the shared "use...a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus...Long Rest" sentence sits in the **first, unlabeled** segment, scanned completely independently of the six option segments (each segment is scanned in isolation by design, scraper.mjs:851-862). Concretely:
  - The intro segment itself builds nothing (no dice/save/spell/status/effect hint fires on it at all), so its correctly-parsed `usesFormula: "@prof"`-equivalent intent (via the `NUMBER_WORDS`/`profRe`-style logic) and `recoveryPeriod: "lr"` are computed and then simply discarded — they never reach any of the six options' own activities. This is a structural gap: a shared preamble that governs every sub-option has no mechanism to propagate into per-bold-label sub-segments. Not tracked by any of issues 001-019.
  - **Cloud's Jaunt** — `BONUS_ACTION_PHRASE_RE` (scraper.mjs:312) correctly detects `activation.type: "bonus"`, but there is no dice/effect/status/save hint anywhere in "you magically teleport up to 30 feet" (no detector for self-teleportation exists at all), so `guessQueueEntries` (effects-builder.mjs:831-967) builds **zero entries** for it — with `preserveUnmatchedLabels: false` in `buildAutoMechanics` (effects-builder.mjs:993-994), the named sub-option vanishes without even a stub.
  - **Fire's Burn** and **Frost's Chill** — `diceRe` (scraper.mjs:490) does pick up `1d10 Fire damage`/`1d6 Cold damage`, building a `damage` Activity each. But `TRIGGER_CLAUSE_RE` (scraper.mjs:320, anchored `^\s*`) never matches their leading "When you hit a target..." clause, because the segment's own joined text (scraper.mjs:859: `els.map(el => el.textContent)`) still starts with the bold label itself ("Fire's Burn (Fire Giant). When you hit...") — the label is stripped into `seg.label` for display purposes only, not removed from the text the anchor scans. So both riders get built as generic `type: "action"` (buildActivityData's own default, effects-builder.mjs:557) with no trigger condition, instead of the on-hit "special" trigger the prose actually describes. This label-vs-anchor interaction is a genuinely new, previously-undocumented gap that silently defeats `TRIGGER_CLAUSE_RE` on **every** bold-labeled sub-option in the whole pipeline, not just here. Frost's Chill's "reduce its Speed by 10 feet" enemy-debuff clause is also completely uncaptured — no detector targets an opponent's stats, only the wielder's own (e.g. `speedMatch` at scraper.mjs:712 requires "speed increases").
  - **Hill's Tumble** — "you can give that target the Prone condition" builds nothing at all: the condition-grant verb list in `statusHints`'s `grantRe` (scraper.mjs:695: `have|has|gain|gains|gaining|are|become|becomes`) doesn't include "give", and there is no detector anywhere for imposing a condition on a *target* rather than the trait's own holder (correctly, since a naive match would otherwise risk misattributing an enemy debuff as a self-grant). Zero entries built.
  - **Stone's Endurance (this option)** — `REACTION_PHRASE_RE` correctly fires (`activation.type: "reaction"`), but the actual "roll 1d12. Add your Constitution modifier..." mitigation never becomes a dice hint: `diceRe`/`diceWithModifierNoTrailingRe` (scraper.mjs:490, 512) both require the modifier clause to chain directly onto the dice term with "+"/"plus"; here a full sentence break ("roll 1d12. Add your Constitution modifier...") breaks that adjacency, so neither pattern matches. No activity or effect is built — the reaction trigger, like Cloud's Jaunt, is computed and discarded.
  - **Storm's Thunder** — `REACTION_PHRASE_RE` and the `within 60 feet` range parser (scraper.mjs:354) both fire correctly, but the "1d8 Thunder damage to that creature" is **silently dropped** by a confirmed, reproducible bug: `diceRe`'s trailing context-capture group is `[A-Za-z][A-Za-z ]{0,25}?` (scraper.mjs:490, 26-character hard cap). "Fire damage to that target" is exactly 26 characters (matches, per Fire's Burn above); "Thunder damage to that creature" is 31 characters — over the cap — so the regex can never reach the terminating period and the whole dice term fails to match. Verified directly: `diceRe` matches "Fire's Burn"'s text but returns zero matches on "Storm's Thunder"'s otherwise structurally-identical text. This is a new, concrete, easily-reproduced gap not covered by any of issues 001-019 — worth its own issue (widen or remove the `{0,25}` cap).
- **Actual (compendium data):** `effects: []`, `activities: {}`, `advancement: {}`. Consistent with staleness for the option-level dice hints that *would* fire (Fire's Burn/Frost's Chill), but the Cloud's Jaunt / Hill's Tumble / Stone's Endurance / Storm's Thunder gaps above mean even a fresh re-import today would come back with at most two of six options' partial mechanics (Fire's Burn, Frost's Chill — and even those with the wrong activation type and no linkage to the shared uses pool).
- **Gap / verdict:** The single most consequential trait in this audit. Three independent, confirmed, previously-undocumented bugs (shared-preamble non-propagation, label-prefix defeating the trigger-clause anchor, and the `{0,25}` dice-context cap) combine so that a re-import today would still leave 4 of 6 Giant Ancestry options with no mechanics at all, and the 2 that do get something get it with the wrong activation shape and no shared limited-use pool.

### Size
- **Prose:** "Your size is Medium (about 7–8 feet tall)."
- **Expected (current code):** Nothing from `scanSegmentText` — there is no "your size is X" prose detector anywhere in scraper.mjs, by design. Size is meant to be handled structurally: `buildSizeAdvancement` (ddb-scraper.mjs:248-265) reads `raceDef.sizeId` directly (not this trait's prose) and attaches a `Size` advancement entry only to the **race item's** own `system.advancement` (ddb-scraper.mjs:340).
- **Actual (compendium data):** `effects: []`, `advancement: {}`. Matches expectation for the trait item.
- **Gap / verdict:** No gap on the trait item itself, but because Goliath has no race item (finding #1), the Medium-size `Size` advancement `buildSizeAdvancement` would otherwise have produced currently has nowhere to attach at all.

### Ability Score Increases (2024)
- **Prose:** "When determining your character's ability scores, increase one score by 2 and a different one by 1, or increase three scores by 1. None of these increases can raise a score above 20."
- **Expected (current code):** Nothing is built, and this is a real code gap, not staleness — confirmed identical to the same finding already documented against Orc. `guessAbilityScoreImprovement` (scraper.mjs:881-910) anchors its sentence match on `/Increase\s+(?:your|one ability score of your choice|two ability scores of your choice)[^.]*\./i` (line 884), the feat/half-feat 2024 boilerplate this function was built and verified against. This species-level phrasing ("increase one **score** by 2..., or increase three **scores** by 1") never contains "ability score of your choice" and doesn't start the sentence with "Increase your", so the regex never matches — confirmed directly: `guessAbilityScoreImprovement` returns `null` on this exact text.
- **Actual (compendium data):** `advancement: {}`. Consistent with the code gap — still empty on a fresh, non-stale re-import.
- **Gap / verdict:** Cross-species gap (not Goliath-specific), reproducing identically on every core 2024 species that shares this standardized PHB phrasing. Not tracked by any of issues 001-019 (issue 009 only wired the *existing* feat-boilerplate detector into `buildAutoMechanics`; it never extended the phrasing itself). The fix is mechanically simple — the "one score by 2 / three scores by 1" phrasing maps to the exact same `{points: 2, cap: 2, locked: []}` shape the existing "two ability scores of your choice by 1" case already builds (scraper.mjs:899); only a second sentence-anchor alternative is needed.

### Mountain Born

- **Expected (current code):** The resistance clause matches directly: `traitRe` (scraper.mjs:582, `/\b(resistance|resistant|...)\s+to\s+...\s+damage\b/gi`) fires on "resistance to cold damage", producing `{key: "system.traits.dr.value", mode: 2, value: "cold"}` — confirmed via direct probe. `guessQueueEntries` wraps it into a permanent Effect. The high-altitude-acclimation clause correctly produces nothing — dnd5e's schema has no concept for this, so "no mechanic" is the right outcome, not a gap.
- **Actual (compendium data):** `effects: []`. Confirms the empty-pack finding.

### Large Form
- **Prose:** "Starting at character level 5, you can change your size to Large as a Bonus Action if you're in a big enough space. This transformation lasts for 10 minutes or until you end it... For that duration, you have Advantage on Strength checks, and your Speed increases by 10 feet. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest."
- **Expected (current code):** Several sub-signals fire correctly in isolation, but they never combine into anything resembling the real mechanic:
  - `activation.type`: `"bonus"` (correct, via `BONUS_ACTION_PHRASE_RE`).
  - `usesFormula`/`recoveryPeriod`: `"1"` / `"lr"` (correct, via the bare-"once" + Long Rest detector, scraper.mjs:447-456).
  - `effectHints`: `speedMatch` (scraper.mjs:712, "speed increases by N feet") fires on "your Speed increases by 10 feet", producing `{key: "system.attributes.movement.walk", mode: 2 (ADD), value: "10"}`.
  - **The bug:** because the only hint that fires for this segment is an `effectHints` entry (no dice/save/spell/status/toggle hint), `guessQueueEntries` (effects-builder.mjs:900-913) builds this as a **permanent, `transfer: true`** ActiveEffect — the same unconditional shape used for a passive trait like Mountain Born's cold resistance. There is no mechanism anywhere in this pipeline for tying an `effectHints`-only segment's ActiveEffect to the same segment's own `activation`/`usesFormula` (those fields are only ever read when a save/damage/heal/cast/toggle-status Activity is independently built first — the same "computed, then silently discarded" pattern already documented for Orc's Relentless Endurance). Net result on a fresh re-import: Goliaths would get a **permanent +10 ft walking speed bonus, always on**, with no Bonus Action, no 1/Long-Rest limit, and no temporary-Large-size aspect represented anywhere.
  - "Advantage on Strength checks" is entirely uncaptured — no detector anywhere in `scanSegmentText` recognizes an "Advantage on \[Ability\] checks" grant.
  - The level-5 gate itself ("Starting at character level 5") is not enforced by anything in `scanSegmentText`/`guessFeatureMechanics" — it is presumably handled upstream by DDB's own `trait.requiredLevel` field bucketing this trait into the race's level-5 `ItemGrant` (ddb-scraper.mjs:218-232, `importDdbRace` at importer.mjs:950-951), which is outside what this JSON snapshot can confirm, but is at least structurally the correct place for it (assuming DDB reports it).
- **Actual (compendium data):** `effects: []`, `activities: {}`. Matches expectation, but not in a reassuring way — a fresh re-import would trade "nothing" for "one wrong permanent effect," which is arguably worse for a GM who trusts the auto-import without reviewing it.
- **Gap / verdict:** Confirmed new, significant gap. The core problem — no way to gate an `effectHints`-derived Effect on the same segment's own detected Activity/uses/duration — is architectural, not a one-line regex fix, and is worth its own issue.

### Stone's Endurance (standalone 2014-style trait)
- **Prose:** "You can focus yourself to occasionally shrug off injury. When you take damage, you can use your reaction to roll a d12. Add your Constitution modifier to the number rolled and reduce the damage by that total. After you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest."
- **Expected (current code):** `REACTION_PHRASE_RE` correctly fires on "use your reaction" (`activation.type: "reaction"`), and `recoveryPeriod` resolves to `"lr"` — but here it's silently *wrong* in a way that's already a documented scanner quirk (test comment in `test/dc-formula-and-uses-recovery.test.mjs:91-99`): the text says "a short or long rest," which never contains the literal substring "short rest" (it's "short **or long** rest"), so `srIdx` stays `-1` and only the long-rest branch ever matches — the trait is misclassified as long-rest-only when it should recover on either. But none of this ends up mattering, because the actual damage-mitigation mechanic is never captured at all, for **two independent, compounding reasons**:
  1. The dice term itself is written as "a d12", not "1d12" — every dice regex in this file (`diceRe`, `diceWithModifierNoTrailingRe`) requires the literal shape `\d+d\d+` (an explicit leading digit count). "a d12" has no leading digit, so it fails to match at the regex level before adjacency is even considered — confirmed directly (`/\d+d\d+/i.test("...roll a d12...")` returns `false`).
  2. Even if it were written "1d12", the modifier clause ("Add your Constitution modifier...") is a separate sentence, not a "+"/"plus"-chained continuation of the dice term — the same adjacency requirement that defeats Giant Ancestry's own Stone's Endurance option above.
  - Because no dice/save/heal/spell/status hint ever fires, `guessQueueEntries` never builds an Activity, so the correctly-detected `activation`/`usesFormula`(itself wrongly computed as `"@abilities.con.mod"` — the ability-modifier `usesFormula` detector at scraper.mjs:439-441 greedily matches *any* "Constitution modifier" mention anywhere in the segment, including this one which describes the damage-reduction bonus, not a limited-use count; it happens to be harmless here only because it's never attached to anything)/`recoveryPeriod` are all computed and then discarded — the exact same "computed, then silently dropped" pattern already documented for Orc's Relentless Endurance.
- **Actual (compendium data):** `effects: []`, `activities: {}`. Matches expectation.
- **Gap / verdict:** Confirmed "no detector covers this" case, worse than Giant Ancestry's own Stone's Endurance option (which at least fails only on the sentence-break adjacency, not the missing-leading-digit bare-die phrasing too). Also demonstrates the latent `usesFormula` ability-modifier-greediness bug (harmless here, but would silently corrupt `uses.max` on any other feature that combines a damage-reduction ability-modifier mention with genuine short/long-rest recovery language and *does* otherwise trigger an Activity). Worth a follow-up issue: (a) accept bare `a/an d\d+` dice phrasing, (b) allow a modifier clause across a sentence break when it's the very next sentence, (c) scope the `usesFormula` ability-modifier match more narrowly (e.g. require "number of times equal to" nearby) so it can't misfire on an unrelated ability-modifier mention elsewhere in the same segment.

### Ability Score Increase (2014)
- **Prose:** "Your Strength score increases by 2, and your Constitution score increases by 1."
- **Expected (current code):** Nothing is built — a second, independent miss from the 2024-phrasing gap above. `guessAbilityScoreImprovement`'s anchor (scraper.mjs:884) requires the sentence to *start* with "Increase" (imperative); "Your Strength score increases by 2..." is declarative and never matches, confirmed via direct probe (`guessAbilityScoreAdvancement` returns `null`). This is, ironically, the *easiest* of the three ASI phrasings in this dataset to parse correctly — a clean, unconditional "X score increases by N, and Y score increases by M" — but the current detector's imperative-only anchor rejects it outright.
- **Actual (compendium data):** `advancement: {}`. Consistent with the code gap.
- **Gap / verdict:** New finding, distinct from (but sharing a root cause with) the 2024-phrasing gap above: the current ASI detector only recognizes the feat/half-feat "Increase..." imperative family, and neither the 2024 species phrasing nor this 2014 declarative phrasing is covered. See Species-level notes for why this legacy trait shows up at all alongside the 2024 one.

- **Actual (compendium data):** `effects: []`. Matches expectation.

## Species-level notes

- **Race item absence confirmed for Goliath** (`raceItemExists: false`), consistent with the pack-wide finding across all 9 core PHB 2024 species (vs. the 7 non-core species that *did* get race items in the same batch). Static review of `assembleRaceItem`/`buildSizeAdvancement`/`mergeTraitDerivedMovement` (ddb-scraper.mjs) turns up no obvious throw path for a normal Goliath `raceDef` shape, and `importDdbRace` (importer.mjs:929-963) wraps the final `createItemFromData(assembleRaceItem(...), ...)` call in the same try/catch as trait creation, with each trait individually `await`ed and committed *before* that final step runs — so an exception on the race-item step specifically would explain exactly this pattern (traits persist, race item doesn't, no code path special-cases "core" species by name anywhere in the pipeline). None of the per-trait prose gaps found above can throw (they all fail closed to `null`/no-op), so they aren't candidates for the actual exception. Root-causing it further would need the console/log output from the actual 2026-08-24 run, not available for static review here.
- **Downstream effect of the missing race item:** because `buildSizeAdvancement` and movement/speed both attach only to the race item, Goliath currently has no `Size` advancement and no movement/speed data anywhere in this compendium — not because Speed/Size's own detectors are broken (they're inert by design), but because the one item they'd attach to was never persisted.
- **Duplicate-trait finding — two separate pairs, not one:** Goliath's 12 traits actually cover only 10 distinct trait *concepts*, because both **Powerful Build** and **Ability Score Increase(s)** appear twice with genuinely different text (confirmed by reading `descriptionHtml` directly, not just matching names):
  - "Powerful Build" (identifier `powerful-build`, 2024 text: carrying capacity only) vs. a second "Powerful Build" (identifier also `powerful-build` — same `slugify` collision, since `identifier` is derived purely from the name) whose text adds "Advantage on any ability check... to end the Grappled condition" — the 2014-ruleset Elemental Evil Player's Companion version of the trait.
  - "Ability Score Increases" (identifier `ability-score-increases`, 2024 flexible "+2/+1 or +1/+1/+1" boilerplate) vs. "Ability Score Increase" (identifier `ability-score-increase`, singular — 2014's fixed "+2 Strength, +1 Constitution").
  - This is the same anomaly already documented on Orc (Adrenaline Rush 2024 + Relentless Endurance 2014 coexisting), but doubled: Goliath has **two** independent 2014/2024 trait collisions in the same import, stronger evidence that this is a systemic data-provenance issue in how D&D Beyond's `racialTraits` payload (or this account's ruleset-preference setting) surfaces legacy trait definitions, not a one-off. `usableRacialTraits` (ddb-scraper.mjs:165-169) filters only on `hideInSheet`; there is no per-trait `isLegacy`/ruleset check anywhere in the import path — `buildRaceTraitItemData`'s own `isLegacy` flag (ddb-scraper.mjs:182-209) is a single value taken from the whole `raceDef`, applied identically to every trait regardless of which ruleset that specific trait's text actually belongs to. Both duplicate pairs share nearly-identical `createdTime` values (within ~5ms of the rest of the batch), so this is not two separate import runs merged together — it's the same single `importDdbRace` call receiving duplicate/legacy-plus-current entries in `raceDef.racialTraits` and building an item for every one of them with no dedup step.
  - Worth flagging to engineering as its own issue: a per-trait ruleset filter (or at minimum a duplicate-name warning in the import report) in `usableRacialTraits`.
- **Giant Ancestry as the richest choice-modeling case in the set:** as detailed above, unlike Dragonborn's Draconic Ancestry (flavor + a single damage type substituted into otherwise-uniform mechanics), Giant Ancestry's six options are mechanically *distinct* shapes (a teleport, two on-hit damage riders, a condition-imposition, and two damage-mitigation/retaliation reactions) sharing one limited-use pool — and the current pipeline's per-segment, no-cross-segment-propagation design means it captures at most 2 of the 6 correctly-shaped mechanics (Fire's Burn, Frost's Chill damage dice), both attached with the wrong activation type and no shared uses budget, even on a fully fresh, non-stale re-import. This is the strongest single argument in this audit for extending the pipeline beyond independent per-block scanning toward at least optionally inheriting a preceding unlabeled segment's uses/recovery context into subsequent labeled sub-option segments.
- **Cross-species implication:** the Ability Score Increases gap (both the 2024 flexible phrasing and, uniquely surfaced here via the duplicate legacy trait, the 2014 fixed phrasing) is structural, not Goliath-specific, and reproduces identically for every other core species sharing the same PHB boilerplate — none of their ASI grants would import correctly even on a fresh, non-stale run today.
