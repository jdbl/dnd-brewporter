# Species Audit: Human

## Summary

No `race`-type Item exists for Human in the compendium (`raceItemExists: false`), consistent with the cross-species finding that all 9 core PHB 2024 species failed to get a race item while 7 non-core species succeeded. All 9 Human trait items have completely empty `effects`/`activities`/`advancement` — byte-for-byte the placeholder shape `buildRaceTraitItemData` initializes (`scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:200-202`), not "scanned and found nothing." This is consistent with the import (2026-08-24 ~16:52 UTC) predating every commit that wires `buildAutoMechanics`/proficiency/ASI detection into the species import path (all dated 2026-08-25 in `git log`). Re-running the import against current code would fix **Skillful** (a correct skill-choice `Trait` advancement) but would still leave **7 of 9 traits mechanically empty** even under current HEAD, for two different reasons: some traits are genuinely non-mechanical prose with no detector gap (Creature Type, Speed, Versatile's flavor framing), and some expose real, previously-undocumented detector gaps — most notably a **duplicate/conflicting Ability Score Increase trait pair** that neither current phrasing-detector recognizes at all, and a **language-choice grant that silently drops the "of your choice" half** of Languages. Both are new findings not covered by issues 001-019.

## Traits

### Size
- **Prose:** "Your Size is Medium (about 4–7 feet tall) or Small (about 2–4 feet tall), chosen when you select this species."
- **Expected (current code):** Nothing from this trait *item* itself — no detector in `scanSegmentText` (`scripts/scraper.mjs:393-800`) matches "Your Size is..." phrasing (it isn't a speed/sense/resistance/condition/dice pattern), so `buildAutoMechanics` produces empty effects/activities/advancement here regardless of code version. The real Size mechanic is meant to live on the *race item* via `buildSizeAdvancement` (`scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:248-265`), which is species-level, not per-trait — see Species-level notes below.
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty, as expected — this is "inherently non-mechanical for the trait item," not a detector miss.
- **Gap / verdict:** No gap on the trait item. The real mechanic (Size advancement) never got attached anywhere because the race item itself was never created (finding #1).

### Languages
- **Prose:** "You can speak, read, and write Common and one extra language of your choice. Humans typically learn the languages of other peoples..."
- **Expected (current code):** `guessProficiencyAdvancement`'s language regex (`scripts/scraper.mjs:970-972`, `/\bspeak,?\s*read,?\s*(?:and|&)\s*write\s+([^.;]+?).../gi`) matches and captures "Common and one extra language of your choice", which is passed to `parseLanguageGrants` (`scripts/scraper.mjs:1029-1041`). That function only does an exact-name lookup against `LANGUAGE_CODES` (`scripts/scraper.mjs:70-79`) after splitting on comma/and/or — "Common" resolves to `languages:standard:common` and is granted, but "one extra language of your choice" fails the lookup and is **silently dropped with no trace**, unlike the analogous skill/tool wildcard handling (`skillWildcardMatch` at `scripts/scraper.mjs:1121-1124`, `TOOL_CATEGORY_WORDS` used in `parseToolProficiencies`) which does recognize "N skill(s) of your choice." Net result under current HEAD: a `Trait` advancement granting Common only, with **no choice entry for the extra language** — a partial, silently-incomplete grant, not "nothing" and not fully correct.
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty (stale — predates the 2026-08-25 proficiency-clause-parser commit `75fccb3` entirely).
- **Gap / verdict:** Two layered issues. (1) Staleness explains the current empty state. (2) Independently, even after a fresh reimport on current HEAD, the result would be **incomplete** — this is a new gap: `parseLanguageGrants` has no wildcard/"of your choice" pool support analogous to what skills and tools already have. Not covered by issue 005 (which only added literal named-language detection) or any other issue 001-019.

### Versatile
- **Prose:** "You gain an Origin feat of your choice."
- **Expected (current code):** No detector matches this phrasing at all. `guessProficiencyAdvancement`'s clause regex requires the word "proficiency" or "training" (`scripts/scraper.mjs:957`), which isn't present. There is no ItemChoice/feat-grant detector anywhere in `scraper.mjs` — a search for "Origin feat," "feat of your choice," and "ItemChoice" across `scraper.mjs`/`effects-builder.mjs` returns nothing; `ItemChoice` only appears in `ddb-scraper.mjs` (for a class's own feature-choice tables) and in the (unmerged) issue 018 fix for `copyOfficialFeatMechanics`, neither of which applies to race-trait prose scanning.
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty.
- **Gap / verdict:** No detector matches this phrasing — genuine, previously undocumented gap. Granting a feat from prose would require a new "feat of your choice" → `ItemChoice` advancement detector that doesn't exist yet, distinct from every existing skill/tool/language/ASI detector. Not covered by any of issues 001-019.

### Skillful
- **Prose:** "You gain proficiency in one skill of your choice."
- **Expected (current code):** Matches cleanly. `guessProficiencyAdvancement`'s clause regex (`scripts/scraper.mjs:957`) captures "one skill of your choice," and `parseProficiencyClause`'s wildcard branch (`scripts/scraper.mjs:1121-1124`, `skillWildcardMatch`) — whose own comment explicitly cites "the real shape dnd5e's own Human Skillful trait uses" (`scripts/scraper.mjs:1088`) — produces `choices: [{ count: 1, pool: ["skills:*"] }]`, wrapped into a `Trait` advancement (`scripts/scraper.mjs:977-984`). `scanSegmentText` contributes nothing further (no dice/save/sense/condition language), so no effects/activities.
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty.
- **Gap / verdict:** Pure staleness — this trait is explicitly the reference case the current detector was built and tested against. A fresh reimport on current HEAD would correctly populate this trait's `advancement` with a skill-choice `Trait` entry.

### Ability Score Increases
- **Prose:** "When determining your character's ability scores, increase one score by 2 and a different one by 1, or increase three scores by 1. None of these increases can raise a score above 20."
- **Expected (current code):** No ASI advancement is produced. `guessAbilityScoreImprovement` (`scripts/scraper.mjs:881-910`) anchors its sentence-extraction regex on `Increase\s+(?:your|one ability score of your choice|two ability scores of your choice)` (`scripts/scraper.mjs:884`) — a family of phrasings drawn from feat/Epic-Boon boilerplate ("Increase your Constitution...", "Increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or increase two ability scores of your choice by 1..."). Human's actual species-level phrasing — "increase **one score** by 2 and a different one by 1, or increase **three scores** by 1" — never contains the literal substrings the regex alternation requires ("one ability score of your choice" / "two ability scores of your choice" / "your"), so `sentence` is `undefined` and the function returns `null`. `guessProficiencyAdvancement` also doesn't apply (no proficiency/training/language language present).
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty.
- **Gap / verdict:** Genuine detector gap, not (only) staleness — even on current HEAD this trait would still produce nothing. The species-level 2024 ASI phrasing is a materially different sentence shape from every feat-level phrasing `guessAbilityScoreImprovement` was written against. Not covered by issue 009 (which only wires the existing detector into `buildAutoMechanics`, doesn't extend its phrasing coverage) or any other issue 001-019 — this is a new finding worth its own issue.

### Speed
- **Prose:** "Your Speed is 30 feet."
- **Expected (current code):** No effect is produced. `scanSegmentText`'s three speed regexes all require either a delta ("speed increases by/to N feet," `scripts/scraper.mjs:712,724`) or an absolute grant phrased as "you have a `<type>` speed of N feet" naming a specific movement type (`scripts/scraper.mjs:736`). "Your Speed is 30 feet" matches none of these shapes (no "increases," no "you have a," no named movement-type word). Separately, `mergeTraitDerivedMovement`'s own `ABSOLUTE_SPEED_GRANT` regex (`scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:273`) has the identical "you have a/an `<type>` speed of" requirement and also would not match — but that function only runs against the race item's assembly (`importDdbRace`, `scripts/importer.mjs:955`), which never happens for Human anyway.
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty.
- **Gap / verdict:** No gap for the trait item — base walking speed for a species is meant to come from `raceDef.weightSpeeds?.normal` on the race item (`assembleRaceItem`, `scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:326`), not from parsing this flavor-restating trait's own prose. This is inherently redundant flavor text once a race item exists; it's only "missing" here because the race item itself is missing (finding #1).

### Creature Type
- **Prose:** "You are a Humanoid."
- **Expected (current code):** No effect is produced from the trait's own prose — there is no creature-type detector anywhere in `scanSegmentText`. This is intentional: `assembleRaceItem` unconditionally hardcodes `system.type.value: "humanoid"` on the race item (`scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:342-346`), independent of this trait's text, since (per the code's own comment) "playable species are Humanoid unless stated otherwise" under both rulesets.
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty.
- **Gap / verdict:** No gap on the trait item — this is inherently non-mechanical flavor text for a `feat`-type item; the actual mechanic lives on the race item's `system.type.value`, which for Human would in fact be set correctly (to "humanoid") if the race item existed. Another casualty of finding #1, not a detector gap.

### Ability Score Increase
- **Prose:** "Your ability scores each increase by 1."
- **Expected (current code):** No ASI advancement is produced, for the same regex-anchor reason as "Ability Score Increases" above: `guessAbilityScoreImprovement`'s sentence regex (`scripts/scraper.mjs:884`) requires "Increase" immediately followed by "your"/"one ability score of your choice"/"two ability scores of your choice." "Your ability scores each increase by 1" has "increase" appearing after "each," not before "your" in the required order — the literal substring "increase your" never occurs — so no match.
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty.
- **Gap / verdict:** Detector gap (same root cause as "Ability Score Increases"), but also a **species-level data anomaly worth flagging on its own**: this trait's flat "+1 to all six abilities" is the 2014 PHB Human's Ability Score Increase, not 2024 PHB Human's — the two traits describe two different, mutually exclusive ASI rules for the same species. `usableRacialTraits` (`scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:165-169`) filters D&D Beyond's racial traits only on `hideInSheet`, with no rules-version/ruleset-preference filtering, so if D&D Beyond's race definition for Human genuinely carries both a legacy and a 2024 ASI trait definition and neither is marked `hideInSheet`, both get imported as separate, simultaneously-existing trait items. Today this is mechanically inert (neither phrasing is detected — see above), but if the phrasing gap above were fixed without also addressing this, a Human import could end up granting **both** a 2/1-split ASI and a flat +1-to-all-six ASI to the same character. Not covered by any of issues 001-019.

### Resourceful
- **Prose:** "You gain Heroic Inspiration whenever you finish a Long Rest."
- **Expected (current code):** Nothing is produced. `scanSegmentText` sets `recoveryPeriod: "lr"` from the "Long Rest" mention (`scripts/scraper.mjs:432-436`), but that alone never produces a queue entry — `usesFormula`/`recoveryPeriod` only get attached to an activity that's already been built from a saving throw, dice hint, or spell name (`guessQueueEntries`, `scripts/effects-builder.mjs`), none of which are present here. There is no detector anywhere for granting Heroic Inspiration (no keyword/flag match for "Heroic Inspiration" in `scraper.mjs` or `effects-builder.mjs`).
- **Actual (compendium data):** Empty.
- **Gap / verdict:** No detector matches this phrasing — genuine gap. Foundry's dnd5e system tracks Heroic Inspiration as a boolean actor flag rather than an item grant, which none of the existing effect-hint patterns (AC, resistance/immunity, condition, senses, speed, HP-per-level, powerful build) are shaped to produce. Not covered by any of issues 001-019.

## Species-level notes

**Missing race item.** Consistent with the cross-species finding: Human's `raceItemExists: false`. Nothing in `importDdbRace` (`scripts/importer.mjs:929-963`) special-cases "core" vs. "non-core" species by name, so the failure is presumably either an exception during `assembleRaceItem`/`buildSizeAdvancement`/`mergeTraitDerivedMovement` for this raceDef shape, or a `Foundry rejected...`/creation failure that would show up in `report.failed` — that report isn't part of the data available for this audit, so the specific cause for Human couldn't be confirmed here; it would need the actual import report or raw `raceDef` JSON to pin down further.

**Size-choice handling would be correct if the race item existed.** `SIZE_ID_TO_DND5E` (`scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:239`) explicitly maps DDB's `sizeId: 10` to `["sm", "med"]`, and the surrounding comment states this was "confirmed directly against live D&D Beyond API data" and matches "the real `configuration:{sizes:["sm","med"]}` shape official 2024 Human ships with" — i.e., this mapping was written with Human's own size-choice specifically in mind and already produces the correct dnd5e `Size` advancement shape (`buildSizeAdvancement`, `scripts/ddb-scraper.mjs:248-265`) for a Small-or-Medium choice. This is not a bug: the sizeId→dnd5e mapping correctly anticipates and handles Human's "chosen when you select this species" case. The only reason Human doesn't actually get this advancement today is finding #1 (no race item created at all) — there's no independent size-handling bug here.

**New gaps found, not covered by issues 001-019 (worth filing):**
1. Species-level ASI phrasing ("increase one score by 2 and a different one by 1, or increase three scores by 1"; "your ability scores each increase by 1") isn't recognized by `guessAbilityScoreImprovement`'s feat/boon-oriented regex at all — affects every species with this exact 2024 PHB boilerplate, not just Human.
2. `parseLanguageGrants` has no "language of your choice" wildcard/choice-pool handling, unlike the equivalent skill/tool detectors — affects every species/feat granting "and one/N extra language(s) of your choice."
3. No detector exists for "gain an Origin/other feat of your choice" → `ItemChoice` advancement from trait prose.
4. No detector exists for "gain Heroic Inspiration" (or any other actor-flag-shaped grant with no existing effect-hint key).
5. Possible duplicate/conflicting ASI trait definitions (2014-style flat +1-to-all alongside 2024-style 2/1-split) surviving `usableRacialTraits`'s `hideInSheet`-only filter — currently inert since neither phrasing is detected, but would become a real double-grant bug if gap #1 above were fixed without also addressing this.
